# Change request CD-21: email notifications

- **Ticket:** CD-21 "Email notifications on new tickets and replies" (Epic E5 Notifications
  & SLA, Must, 5 pts)
- **Raised:** mid Sprint 2 (Weeks 4-5), November 2026
- **Status:** accepted, implemented on `feature/CD-21-email-notifications`

## Client message

> "We now need emails on new tickets and replies."

## Scope decision

In scope (this change):

- An email to every active agent and admin when a ticket is created.
- An email on a public reply: to the customer when staff reply, and to the assigned agent
  (or all active agents when unassigned) when the customer replies.
- Internal notes never send an email.
- Best-effort sending: a mail failure never breaks or changes the API response, and is
  logged without addresses or message text.
- Configuration through env vars only, off by default (`EMAIL_ENABLED=false`).
  Details: [`../notifications.md`](../notifications.md).

Out of scope (not requested in the client message, can follow as separate tickets):

- An email on **assignment** (listed on the original CD-21 card, but not in the client's
  message). It reuses the same mailer and adds one event.
- Emails on status changes, per-user opt-out, digests, retries or a mail queue.
- A real ticket number field. The emails show a short reference (`#` and the last 6
  characters of the id).

## Estimate impact

- CD-21 is re-estimated at **5 pts** and added to **Sprint 2: +5 pts** on the committed
  scope.
- The work covers mail settings and startup checks, the mailer and notification rules, two
  route hooks, the tests (recipients, internal notes, failures, header injection, HTML
  escaping) and the docs.
